About Rising Starz

Rising Starz is India's premier academic tutoring institution, dedicated to nurturing young minds and helping them achieve their full potential. Founded in 2012 with a vision to make quality education accessible to all, we have been serving students across various age groups and academic levels for over a decade.

Our journey began in a small classroom with just 15 students and a handful of passionate teachers. Today, we have grown into a network of educational centers across multiple cities, touching the lives of thousands of students annually. Our growth has been fueled by our unwavering commitment to educational excellence and the trust of parents and students who have seen remarkable transformations in their academic performance.

Our mission is to provide personalized attention, innovative teaching methods, and a supportive learning environment that empowers students to excel in their academic pursuits. We believe that every student has unique learning needs, and our approach is tailored to bring out the best in each individual.

With state-of-the-art facilities, experienced faculty, and a comprehensive curriculum, Rising Starz has become a trusted name in academic coaching across India. Our success is measured not just by the grades our students achieve, but by the confidence they gain and the lifelong learning skills they develop.

Frequently Asked Questions

📚 What courses do you offer?

We offer comprehensive coaching for Class 6-12 in Mathematics, Science, English, and Social Studies, along with specialized preparation for competitive exams like JEE, NEET, and board examinations.

💰 What is the fee structure?

Our fees vary based on the course, batch timing, and duration. We offer flexible payment options including monthly, quarterly, and annual payments. Scholarships are available for meritorious students.

👥 How do I enroll?

You can enroll by filling out the registration form on our website, visiting any of our branches, or calling our admissions helpline. Our team will guide you through the process.

📅 Can I change my batch timing?

Yes, subject to availability. We understand that schedules may change, and we try to accommodate batch change requests within the first month of enrollment.

🎓 Do you provide study materials?

Yes, we provide comprehensive study materials including textbooks, practice papers, mock tests, and online resources. All materials are included in the course fee.

🏆 What is your success rate?

We maintain a 95% success rate with our students consistently scoring above 90% in board exams. Over 200 of our students have secured top ranks in various competitive examinations.
